    Architectural Flow

    How Our Agents Collaborate

    Unlike monolithic prompts, Aetheria orchestrates an ensemble of autonomous specialist agents.

    01

    Preferences & Goals

    Your budget, travel dates, style, and culinary constraints are sanitized and bound to a formal planning state.

    02

    Geospatial Research

    The destination agent resolves exact coordinates via Nominatim and analyzes seasonal climatic trends.

    03

    Parallel Specialist Agents

    Ten workers run concurrently: fetching live Open-Meteo forecasts, querying Overpass places, and calculating budget splits.

    04

    Itinerary Synthesis

    The itinerary agent weaves attractions, restaurants, and transit times into balanced morning, afternoon, and evening blocks.

    05

    Refine & Export

    Review your interactive dashboard, consult the assistant, export to RFC 5545 calendar, or generate a PDF.

    Engineering Highlights

    Production-Ready Capabilities

    Every feature represents genuine backend infrastructure and honest data provenance.

    🛡️

    Verified Places Only

    Attractions and restaurants originate from real OpenStreetMap nodes, Overpass queries, or explicitly badged AI suggestions.

    🌦️

    Live Weather Forecasts

    Integrated with Open-Meteo for real 16-day forecasts, or historical climate models for trips further in advance.

    💰

    Realistic Budget Allocation

    Distributes your total budget proportionally across stays, food, activities, and local transit with an interactive SVG chart.

    ⚡

    Real-Time SSE Streaming

    Server-Sent Events stream pipeline milestones to your browser with automatic 2-second polling fallbacks.

    📅

    RFC 5545 Calendar Sync

    Export native .ics calendar events for morning, afternoon, and evening blocks directly into Apple, Google, or Outlook calendar.

    🔒

    Strict Client Privacy

    Saved itineraries remain strictly local to your browser. Shared links use public share slugs with zero origin exposure.

    My Saved Expeditions

    Trips saved locally on this browser. Private trip IDs are never exposed.
