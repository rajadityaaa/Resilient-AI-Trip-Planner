Proven Architecture

Everything In One Connected Plan

Real capabilities engineered for genuine travelers. Zero fabricated claims.

⚡

Parallel Multi-Agent Architecture

Rather than a single monolithic prompt, Aetheria dispatches ten specialized agents concurrently—each dedicated to geocoding, meteorological forecasts, budget mathematics, or dining curation.

🗺️

Google Maps-Style Interface

Vendored Leaflet map with crisp Esri vector roads, satellite imagery, SVG teardrop pins, and direct links to Google Maps.

⛅

Live Open-Meteo Integration

True 16-day daily forecasts calculating precipitation probability, temperature ranges, and weather codes to adapt day schedules.

💰

Transparent Budget Split

Mathematical proportion allocation across accommodations, food, transit, and contingency buffers with clear estimates.

📅

Instant Share & Calendar Export

Export full itineraries to RFC 5545 `.ics` calendars (Apple, Google, Outlook) and generate permanent private share links.

Architectural Flow

How Our Agents Collaborate

Unlike monolithic prompts, Aetheria orchestrates an ensemble of autonomous specialist agents.

01

Preferences & Goals

Your budget, travel dates, style, and culinary constraints are sanitized and bound to a formal planning state.

02

Geospatial Research

The destination agent resolves exact coordinates via Nominatim and analyzes seasonal climatic trends.

03

Parallel Specialist Agents

Ten workers run concurrently: fetching live Open-Meteo forecasts, querying Overpass places, and calculating budget splits.

04

Itinerary Synthesis

The itinerary agent weaves attractions, restaurants, and transit times into balanced morning, afternoon, and evening blocks.

05

Refine & Export

Review your interactive dashboard, consult the assistant, export to RFC 5545 calendar, or generate a PDF.
